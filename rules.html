The rules · privacy · transparency

PLAY FAIR.
STAY CLEAR.

BLACK JEE is a learning challenge. Read how attempts, results, rankings and optional public posts work.

1. One attempt per account

  • Sign in with your Google account before starting.
  • Each signed-in account can submit one answer per scheduled challenge. The database enforces this rule.
  • Start the timer before selecting and submitting your option. Submissions are checked by the server.
  • Do not share your account or try to manipulate the timer or leaderboard.

2. Answers, results and official solutions

  • After submitting, you see only whether your answer is correct or incorrect.
  • The correct option and worked solution stay hidden until the challenge's official results release at 6:00 PM IST on its result date.
  • Challenges open at 12:00 PM IST on the day before the displayed result date and close at 12:00 PM IST on that date.
  • For the top three, correct submissions are ranked by score, then the shortest server-recorded elapsed time, then submission time.
  • Results and archives can be delayed if a challenge needs review or has no published answer.

3. Golden Sunday

Every scheduled Sunday challenge is titled THE SOVEREIGN GAUNTLET — GOLDEN SUNDAY. It follows the same one-attempt rule and result-release time. A real question must be prepared and scheduled by the challenge owner in advance.

4. Privacy and public names

  • Your attempt history is tied to your signed-in account and is not shown as a public profile.
  • Your display name appears in the public winners list only if you consent and the entry is approved.
  • Do not upload private information in a solution photo. Photos are optional and may be reviewed before publication.
  • BLACK JEE does not display your Google email in the public winners list. After results time, use the Challenge Archive to find the official correct option, any published worked solution, and confirmed winners.
  • You can leave the public-name consent unchecked and still submit your answer.

5. Learning-first policy

BLACK JEE is an educational challenge. A leaderboard position is not an official JEE score or rank. No cash prize or reward is promised unless a separate, clear announcement says otherwise. Report incorrect questions or result issues to the site owner before relying on a result.

6. Respect and accuracy

Use your own work, respect other students, and do not post abusive or misleading content. The owner may correct a question or withhold a result if an error or integrity issue is found. Published worked solutions are intended for learning, not as a substitute for checking the underlying concept.
